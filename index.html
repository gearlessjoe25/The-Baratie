⚓ Welcome to Baratie – The Floating Feast of the Grand Line ⚓

No shirt? No shoes? No bounty? No problem. Just bring your appetite and respect our the house rules.

  • We believe in cooking with passion, pride, and a pinch of peril. Every dish is crafted with care, from sea-salted appetizers to sky-high soufflés. The heat of our kitchen matches the fire of our crew—our chefs are as skilled with blades as they are with butter.
  • Here, you won’t just eat—you’ll remember. Expect bold flavors, wild stories, and the occasional broken table. That’s just how we do things out here on the open sea.

At Baratie, we don’t just serve food—we serve legends on a plate. Moored on the edge of danger and delight, we provide dignified hospitality to pirates, marines, and wanderers alike. Whether you’re escaping the Marines or sailing toward your dreams, Baratie is your haven of flavor.

The Best Meat in the Grand Line!

Sea King meat is our personal favorite among our diverse delicious line-up.

Since sea kings have been depicted in multiple forms, it's impossible to figure out what their meat can be substituted with: fish, seafood, red meat? My personal favorite is seared salmon. I love how it pairs with salty and crumbly Gorgonzola. But frankly, anything goes, as far as I'm concerned, when it comes to this recipe as long as you've got penne and gorgonz cheese covered.

The Grandlines most Exotic Drink!

A refreshing, colorful sorbet made from a blend of fruit flavors found in ever corner of the grandline

Each flavor represents a different culture, such as the vibrant and tangy orange sorbet from Conomi Island, the zesty fiery red sorbet from Arabasta Kingdom, and the cool, minty sorbet from Drum Island. It's served in a chilled, artistic fruit bowl, and the sorbet shimmers with a touch of edible gold leaf. It’s the perfect dessert for adventurous eaters looking to experience the taste of the Grand Line's wildest fruits
